1. Identify the original source or confirm that the asset was generated in-house.
2. Obtain and preserve the applicable license or permission.
3. Confirm commercial-use rights, modification rights, and redistribution rights.
4. Record required attribution and any license notice.
5. Replace or quarantine assets whose provenance cannot be verified.
